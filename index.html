Your coaster story, kept your way.

Coastory is a roller coaster tracking app for enthusiasts. Every ride, every park day, the photos and the moments — and your own rules about what counts.

Coming to Android and iPhone

Facts stay facts

What you rode and when is recorded exactly as it happened. How you count it is up to you.

Everything is optional

Ratings, rankings, stats — use the ones you care about, ignore the rest. Nothing gets in the way of logging a ride.

Your photos stay on your phone

Coastory never uploads your photos. Ever. Back them up yourself, whenever you like — see the privacy policy.
