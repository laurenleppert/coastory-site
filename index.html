Good to know

Can I bring my old ride list?

Import ridden coasters from a CSV or TSV file, review the matches, then choose when to save. Ratings, dates, notes and photos from that file are not imported.

What can I take out?

Export your ride history as a CSV file. A separate manual Coastory backup includes your photos and which rides they belong to, for restoring in Coastory.

Where do my photos go?

Coastory does not upload the photos you add. They stay on your phone unless you choose to export or share them. Your phone’s own backup or transfer may also carry them.

How do I install on iPhone?

Use the iPhone beta button above. TestFlight guides you through installing it first if needed, then accepting the invitation.
