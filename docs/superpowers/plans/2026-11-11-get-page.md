# Coastory /get implementation plan

> Execution: implement inline, then independent whole-branch review. Lauren explicitly requested autonomous implementation and production deployment.

**Goal:** A permanent QR destination at https://coastory.app/get with both published store choices.

**Architecture:** Static get/index.html, scoped get/get.css, and a tiny deferred get/device.js. Reuse style.css, fonts, icon, and the approved profile screenshots. Native links are the baseline. No framework or runtime dependency.

**Design:** Extend the current cream/plum and coral design. A compact brand lockup, Fraunces headline “Every coaster has a story.”, concise tracker-first copy, two recognizable download badges, and a genuine phone preview. Downloads precede the preview on phones; desktop uses a balanced two-column layout. Minimal About/Privacy/Support footer. Preserve badge artwork, use a surrounding coral indication for the matching platform, keep DOM and keyboard order stable. Unknown devices get equal choices.

**Scope:** Only this page, badge and QR assets, focused behavior tests, reproducibility and maintenance documentation. Production is origin/main served directly by Pages. The divergent local catalog branch and its publish.py workflow are outside scope. No analytics exists, so none is added.

## Review focus

- Reduced/frozen UA strings and iPad desktop mode: heuristic only; both native links always usable.
- Disabled JavaScript: equal links and full page content remain.
- 320px phones, zoom, keyboard focus and contrast: no clipped content; comfortable targets.
- /get redirect and direct /get/ refresh on Pages.
- QR scans exactly https://coastory.app/get with an opaque white quiet zone.

## Decisions

- Existing production workflow wins over unpublished local deployment tooling.
- No new analytics infrastructure. Device inference runs locally and is not saved or sent.
- /get is the QR payload; Pages may normalize the directory to /get/.
- Design choices are implementation judgments under the user’s explicit delegation.
