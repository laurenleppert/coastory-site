Who we are

Coastory is made by Velocicoder Studios. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact support@coastory.app.

The short version

Coastory is a roller coaster tracking app. Your ride history, ratings, notes, and photos live on your phone. Your photos never leave your phone — Coastory has no way to upload them, so we couldn't see them if we wanted to. If you create a Coastory Account, your email address and a text-only copy of your ride history (never photos) are stored on our servers so you can get your history back on a new phone. If the app crashes, a crash report goes to our error-reporting service so we can fix it; that report never contains your photos, notes, or ride history. We don't run ads, we don't have analytics watching what you do in the app, and we don't sell your data to anyone.

What we collect

Information you provide directly

  • Your display name and any profile details you enter. Stored on your phone. Sent to our servers only as part of the history copy described below, and only if you create an Account.
  • Your ride history, ratings, tags, notes, park visits, and your own custom park and coaster records. Stored on your phone as the source of truth. If you create an Account, a text-only copy is kept on our servers so you can bring your history to a new phone. This copy is the records themselves — which coasters, when, what you rated and wrote — and it does not include photos.
  • Your photos. Stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We never upload, transmit, or have access to your photos in any form, whether or not you have an Account. This is how the app is built, not a setting: there is no photo upload path in Coastory. See "Why we don't store your photos" below.
  • Your email address, if you create an Account. Sign-in is passwordless: we email you a one-time code rather than asking you to set a password, so we never see or store a password for your Account.
  • Feedback and issue reports you choose to submit. If you report a catalog error or a bug through the app's "Report an Issue" feature, we receive what you wrote, the screen you were on and the few screens before it, and basic technical context (app version, catalog version, device model, OS version, language setting, light/dark theme), plus an app-generated identifier that lets us tell reports from the same installation apart. This context never includes your photos or ride history unless you type it into the report yourself.

Information collected automatically

  • Crash and stability reports. If Coastory crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent to our error-reporting service, Sentry. A report contains the technical details of the error (the stack trace), your device model, operating system version, app version, and a short trail of app-level events just before the error (for example the app moving to the background or the network connection changing). The app also tells Sentry when a session starts and whether it ended in a crash, so we can see how stable a release is. Reports carry a random per-installation identifier generated by the reporting library; it is not your email, your Account, or any advertising or hardware ID, and we do not connect it to your Account. Reports never include your photos, your notes, your ride history, or a screenshot of the screen — screenshot capture is turned off. We have configured Sentry not to store your IP address. There is currently no in-app switch to turn crash reporting off; it is on in every store build. [LEGAL] confirm whether an opt-out is required in any launch market.
  • Server logs. Our backend provider, Supabase, keeps ordinary infrastructure logs of requests to our servers (for example, that a sign-in code was requested). These are server operations logs, not per-user activity tracking, and they are governed by Supabase's own retention.
  • Nothing else. Coastory has no analytics or advertising SDK. We don't know how many people use the app, which screens are popular, or how long you spend in it. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first, and anything we add will be scoped to counts and screen names — never the content of what you logged, searched, or photographed.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and photo library — only when you choose to take or pick a photo (for a ride, a day at the park, or a park pass). The image stays on your phone. If you turn on "Also save to my Photos" in Settings, the app also writes copies to your own photo library; that setting is off unless you choose it.
  • Internet — for Account sign-in, the optional cloud copy of your history, issue reports you submit, and crash reports.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, or any other permission.

Why we don't store your photos

Coastory's photos are stored only on your phone, by design. Hosting rider-uploaded images on a server would make Coastory a content host with legal obligations (including image-scanning and reporting requirements) that a very small studio isn't equipped to meet responsibly. Rather than take on that obligation and the infrastructure it requires, we simply never take a copy. The trade-off is real: if you lose your phone without a backup, your photos are gone with it. The app's Backup & Restore feature exists so you can take your own copy of your history and your photos off the phone, onto storage you control.

Your phone's own backup

Separately from anything Coastory does, your phone's operating system may back up app data to your Google or Apple account as part of its normal device backup or phone-to-phone transfer. On Android, Coastory allows its database (your ride history) into that backup and excludes photos, so the backup stays small enough to succeed; a direct phone-to-phone transfer carries everything. That data goes to your own Google or Apple account under their terms, not to us — we never see it and can't restore it for you. Coastory doesn't rely on it and makes no promise about it; the in-app Backup feature is the copy you control.

Where your data lives

If you create an Account, your email address and the text-only copy of your ride history are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, in the United States (Ohio, us-east-2). Crash reports are stored with Sentry in the United States. Your photos and everything else stay on your phone.

Who else sees your data

  • Nobody, by default. Coastory has no public profiles, no social feed, no leaderboards visible to other riders, and no way for another rider to see your history unless you personally export and share it (for example, a photo, your profile card, or a backup file you choose to save or send).
  • Our service providers, who process data only to run the app for us: Supabase (Account sign-in and the optional cloud copy of your history) and Sentry (crash reports). Neither is permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't sell it.
  • Us, in the limited sense that issue reports you submit are read by the founder to fix the app and the shared coaster catalog, and crash reports are read to fix crashes. Reports are not readable by other riders.
  • If required by law. We may disclose information if legally required to (for example, in response to a valid subpoena). As a small studio holding very little data, we expect this to be rare.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (email, cloud copy of your history): for as long as your Account exists. Deleting your Account removes it — see below.
  • Issue reports: until we have acted on them, after which they may be kept in summarized form (what was wrong, what we changed) without the identifier.
  • Crash reports: Sentry keeps them for 90 days by default, then deletes them. [FOUNDER] confirm the retention setting on the Sentry plan before publication.
  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, in the app or by uninstalling.

Your choices and rights

  • Using Coastory without an Account. You can use the full core app — logging rides, rating, tagging, taking photos, building your history — without ever creating an Account. Nothing about the core experience requires one, and the app never nags you to create one.
  • Deleting your data on the phone. Settings → Erase everything removes every record and every photo on the phone and returns the app to a fresh install. If your Account is linked, you choose in the same place whether to empty the cloud copy too. Uninstalling the app also removes everything Coastory stored on the phone.
  • Deleting your Account. Settings → Backup & restore → Delete Account deletes your Account, your email address, and every record stored on our servers.   • Exporting your data. Settings → Backup & restore → Back up now gives you a complete copy of your history, including photos, in a file you control, at any time.
  • Correcting your data. Anything you've recorded is yours to edit or delete directly in the app.

Children's privacy

Coastory is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an Account, contact support@coastory.app and we will delete it. Security

Everything sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS). Data on our servers is protected by row-level access rules so that only the signed-in Account that wrote a record can read it. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — which is one more reason we keep as little as we do.

If we make a material change to what we collect or how we use it, we'll update this page and change the "Last updated" date above, and where practical tell you in the app. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
