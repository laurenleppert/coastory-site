# Coastory website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Roller coaster enthusiasts, including people scanning Lauren’s promotional card at a park.

## Product Purpose

Introduce Coastory and help riders download it. Coastory tracks ridden coasters and lets each rider decide what counts as a credit, with optional personal rankings and park-day memories.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Plain HTML/CSS on GitHub Pages at https://coastory.app. The homepage is script-free. The /get page uses only a small progressive enhancement for device convenience; both store links work without it. No automatic store redirects, authentication, forms, or new analytics service.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Coastory’s existing assets and visual language. Real app captures only; no fabricated app UI or rider testimonials.

## Evidence on Hand

CNAME and live Pages configuration confirm the domain. The current homepage supplies both store destinations and approved sample-rider screenshots. style.css owns the existing theme and type tokens.

## Product Principles

- A rider decides what counts.
- Tracking is complete on its own; depth stays optional.
- Keep download choices immediate and accessible.
- Preserve privacy and avoid collecting data without an established purpose and infrastructure.
