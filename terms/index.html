2. What Coastory is

Coastory is a roller coaster tracking app for enthusiasts: you log the coasters you've ridden, rate and tag them, keep the photos and stories from your park days, and — if you choose — create an Account to keep a cloud copy of your history and bring it to a new phone.

Coastory is under active development. We add, change, and sometimes remove features as the product evolves, and especially in early releases and beta things may change, break, or look unfinished. We'll do our best to say so when something significant changes. Coastory ships with a catalog of parks and coasters we compile from public sources. We work to keep it accurate but can't promise it's complete or error-free; you can always record your own version in your collection, which catalog changes never overwrite, and you can report an issue.

Park, ride, manufacturer, and operator names belong to their owners. Coastory uses them to identify what you rode and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any park, manufacturer, or operator unless it says so.

Coastory is a personal log. It doesn't operate rides, assess their safety, or tell you whether to ride; ratings and notes are riders' own opinions.
